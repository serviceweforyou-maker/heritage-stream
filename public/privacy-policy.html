Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Sanatana360 (operated by MANJUNATH ENTERPRISE), we take your privacy and data security seriously. When you use our platform, we collect the following limited information:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, avatar selection, and encrypted passwords when you create a profile or log in with Google OAuth.
  • Subscription & Transaction Identifiers: Order ID, payment reference ID, active plan tier, purchase timestamp, and validity dates. (Note: We never store your raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI MPINs.)
  • Usage & Preferences: Watch history, bookmarks, completed quizzes, and referral link activity.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account and grant immediate access to your purchased passes.
  • Send automated transaction receipts, welcome guides, and expiry/renewal notifications.
  • Credit referral rewards and pass discounts when your invitees subscribe.
  • Ensure platform security and prevent unauthorized access or fraudulent activity.

3. Data Security & Third-Party Processing

All sensitive communications between your device and Sanatana360 are encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3/HTTPS encryption. Payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant certified partners (Cashfree Payments & Razorpay). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to external advertisers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential cookies and browser LocalStorage to remember your login session, dark-mode settings, audio player volume, and active reading progress.

Under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), you have the right to request a copy of your personal data or demand the complete deletion of your account and records. To request account deletion, email service.weforyou@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
