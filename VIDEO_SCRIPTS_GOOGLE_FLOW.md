## 🛠️ Google Flow / AI Video Generation Instructions & Best Practices

1. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio**:
   - **OTT Platform / Web**: `16:9` (3840x2160 4K UHD or 1920x1080 Full HD).
   - **Viral YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels**: `9:16` (1080x1920).
2. **Standard Quality Modifiers to append to prompts**:
   `Cinematic 8k UHD, hyper-realistic, photorealistic textures, volumetric lighting, golden hour illumination, atmospheric depth, octane render, 24fps cinematic film grain, Unreal Engine 5.4 rendering.`
3. **Voiceover Audio (ElevenLabs / Google Cloud TTS)**:
   - **Voice Style**: Deep, resonant, warm, authoritative baritone (e.g., *Adam / Marcus / George* style) or serene female Indian classical narrator (*Priya / Kavya* style).
   - **Pacing**: Deliberate, majestic, 110–130 words per minute with 1.5s dramatic pauses between scene transitions.
