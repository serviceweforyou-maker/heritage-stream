Ancient Indian Mental Math Sutras

SUTRA: EKADHIKENA PURVENA 2-SECOND SQUARING

To square any number ending in 5 (e.g. 35², 75², 95²): Multiply the first digit by (digit + 1) and attach 25 at the end!

Example for 35²: (3 × 4) = 12 → Append 25 → 1225! ⚡
65² = (6 × 7) followed by 25 = 4225 ⚡
SUTRA: ANTYAYOREVA INSTANT MULTIPLICATION

To multiply any 2-digit number by 11 (e.g. 43 × 11): Split the digits (4 and 3), add them (4 + 3 = 7), and place the sum in the middle!

Example for 43 × 11: 4 _ 3 → (4 + 3 = 7) → 473! ⚡
52 × 11 = 5 (5+2) 2 = 572 ⚡
SUTRA: NIKHILAM NAVATASCARAMAM BASE 100 MULTIPLICATION

Multiply numbers close to 100 (e.g. 96 × 97) in seconds by tracking deviations from 100!

96 is (-4), 97 is (-3) → (96 - 3 = 93) & (-4 × -3 = 12) → 9312! ⚡
94 × 98 = 9212 ⚡
